Courses & Training Programs

Discover the academic pathways, key subjects, and practical learning opportunities offered by the EFREI Computer Science Department.

The EFREI Computer Science Department provides students with a strong academic foundation in computing, software development, and digital technologies. Through a combination of theory, hands-on practice, and project-based learning, students build the technical and professional skills required by modern careers in computer science.

Overview section

A complete learning journey

Our programs are designed to guide students from fundamental concepts to advanced specialisation. Throughout their studies, learners explore programming, data structures, databases, networks, web technologies, software engineering, and emerging fields such as artificial intelligence and cybersecurity.

Program cards

Card 1

Undergraduate Foundations

Students begin by developing essential skills in programming, algorithms, mathematics for computing, and computer systems. This stage helps them understand the core principles that support all later technical learning.

Card 2

Advanced Computer Science

As students progress, they address more advanced topics such as object-oriented design, databases, human-computer interaction, operating systems, web development, and application architecture. These courses prepare them for real-world problem solving and team-based development.

Card 3

Specialisation & Professional Growth

In the final stage of the program, students deepen their expertise through projects, internships, and specialisation modules. They strengthen both their technical mastery and professional readiness for careers in industry or further study.
