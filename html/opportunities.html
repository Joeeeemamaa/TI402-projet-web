Academic & Career Opportunities

Where education meets experience. Discover how EFREI transforms your potential into a tech career through internships, research, and industry connections.

We believe the best education happens when classroom learning meets real-world challenges. That's why every year at EFREI includes mandatory internships, industry-sponsored projects, and opportunities to work alongside faculty on cutting-edge research. Your education isn't delayed until graduation — it starts immediately.

More Than Just a Degree

Real experience, real connections, real careers

EFREI's industry connections aren't superficial — they're deep, ongoing relationships that benefit students at every level. From freshman-year exploratory internships to senior-year technical roles at prestigious companies, the progression is designed to build confidence, competence, and professional networks that last well beyond graduation.

Three Paths to Your Future

1

Internships & Work Placements

Every year includes a mandatory internship — starting from your first year with exploratory roles and building toward substantive technical positions. Our 40+ industry partners actively recruit EFREI students because they trust the quality of our graduates. Past interns have converted their internships into full-time offers at companies like Amazon, BNP Paribas, and Dassault Systèmes.

2

Research & Innovation

For students drawn to deeper inquiry, EFREI offers pathways into active research projects alongside faculty members. Contribute to published papers, participate in international conferences, and explore whether graduate studies might be your path. Our research-active students have presented at venues across Europe and North America.

3

Entrepreneurship & Industry Projects

Have a business idea? We support it. EFREI's startup incubator provides mentorship, workspace, and seed funding for student-founded companies. Meanwhile, our industry project program matches student teams with real company challenges — you'll build something that actually ships while earning course credit.
