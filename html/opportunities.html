Academic & Career Opportunities

Explore the academic pathways, internship programs, and career options available to EFREI Computer Science students.

The EFREI Computer Science program opens doors to a wide range of professional and academic futures. Whether students aim for careers in industry, research, or entrepreneurship, the department provides the tools, networks, and experiences to succeed.

A pathway built for progression

From studies to career

The training pathway is structured to ease the transition from academic learning to professional practice. As they progress through the program, students gain increasing exposure to industry through projects, internships, partner companies, and a rich alumni network. Every stage of the curriculum is designed to build career readiness and professional confidence.

Opportunity cards

Card 1

Internships & Work Placements

Students complete mandatory internships each year, with placements in top companies across France and internationally. These experiences provide real-world skills and build professional networks early in the career journey.

Card 2

Research & Innovation

High-achieving students can engage in research projects alongside faculty, participate in hackathons, or pursue academic publication. This pathway prepares students for Master's, PhD, or R&D careers.

Card 3

Entrepreneurship & Industry Partnerships

EFREI supports student entrepreneurs through incubator access and startup mentoring. Industry partnerships provide additional project opportunities, guest lectures, and direct recruitment pipelines into leading technology companies.

Where graduates go

EFREI Computer Science graduates pursue careers across a wide range of sectors:

Software & Web Development

  • Frontend / Backend Developer
  • Full-Stack Engineer
  • Mobile App Developer

Data & AI

  • Data Scientist
  • Machine Learning Engineer
  • Data Analyst

Systems & Infrastructure

  • DevOps / Cloud Engineer
  • Network Administrator
  • Cybersecurity Analyst

Leadership & Management

  • IT Project Manager
  • CTO / Technical Lead
  • Product Manager

Further study options

Graduates may continue their academic journey through:

  • Master's programs in Computer Science, AI, or Cybersecurity
  • Doctoral research at partner universities
  • Exchange programs with international institutions
  • Professional certifications (AWS, Google Cloud, Cisco, etc.)
