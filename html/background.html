Skills & Background

Explore the technical, analytical, and professional skills students develop throughout the EFREI Computer Science program.

The department focuses on building both hard and soft skills to prepare students for real-world challenges. This page presents the main areas of competency developed across the curriculum, from core programming to advanced applied domains.

A combination of technical and professional growth

What students learn

The program is designed not only to teach tools and concepts, but also to develop the habits of a professional engineer: rigorous thinking, clean communication, and the ability to learn and adapt in a fast-changing digital world. Students learn to approach problems logically, communicate clearly with both technical and non-technical audiences, and collaborate effectively in teams.

Main areas of study

We continuously design our study topics that reflect the diversity and evolution of computer science as a discipline.

Category 1 – Fundamentals

  • Introduction to programming (Python, C)
  • Data structures and algorithms
  • Computer architecture
  • Discrete mathematics

Category 2 – Core CS

  • Object-oriented programming (Java)
  • Databases and SQL
  • Operating systems
  • Computer networks

Category 3 – Applied Computing

  • Web development (HTML, CSS, JS)
  • Software engineering
  • Mobile development
  • Cloud computing

Category 4 – Advanced Topics

  • Artificial intelligence & machine learning
  • Cybersecurity
  • Data science
  • Distributed systems

Category 5 – Professional Practice

  • Internship & industry projects
  • Agile & DevOps practices
  • Technical English
  • Career preparation

How the curriculum progresses

The academic pathway is structured so that the foundation is built first, with increasing complexity each year:

  1. Year 1–2: core concepts and programming basics
  2. Year 3: advanced theory, projects, and teamwork
  3. Year 4–5: specialisation and professional readiness
