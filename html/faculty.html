Faculty

Meet the teaching staff, discover their academic roles, and explore the expertise that supports learning within the EFREI Computer Science Department.

The faculty of the EFREI Computer Science Department plays a central role in the academic journey of students. Through teaching, guidance, and subject expertise, faculty members help create a learning environment that encourages curiosity, rigor, and professional growth.

A team dedicated to academic excellence

Overview section

The department brings together educators and academic professionals with diverse backgrounds in computer science and digital technologies. Their complementary expertise allows students to benefit from a broad and enriching educational experience across multiple areas of study.
