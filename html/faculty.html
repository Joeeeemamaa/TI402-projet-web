The People Behind Your Education

Meet the instructors and researchers who bring decades of industry experience and cutting-edge research into the classroom — committed to your success.

Our faculty aren't just teachers — they're practitioners who've built real systems, published influential research, and continue to push boundaries in their fields. They bring case studies from their work directly into lectures, making abstract concepts tangible and relevant. When you ask "when will I ever use this?", they can show you exactly where they've used it themselves.

Small Classes, Real Connections

The EFREI difference

We keep class sizes manageable so instructors actually know your name. Office hours aren't bureaucratic obligations — they're conversations where you can dig deep into topics, debate approaches, and get career advice from people who've been where you're going. Many students maintain relationships with their professors well after graduation.

How Faculty Support Your Journey

▶

Teaching Excellence

Our instructors combine rigorous academic standards with practical industry insights. They design curriculum around real-world applications, mentor student projects, and continuously update materials to reflect what's actually happening in tech companies right now.

▶

Active Research

Faculty members maintain active research programs in AI, cybersecurity, distributed systems, and software engineering. This means you're learning from people who are contributing to their fields — not just teaching from textbooks written years ago.

▶

Open Door Policy

Beyond scheduled office hours, many faculty maintain an open door philosophy — if their office is open and they're not in a meeting, you can stop by with questions. This accessibility builds mentorship relationships that often continue throughout your career.

Meet the Teaching Staff

These instructors teach across multiple years and subjects, giving them a broad view of your development as a programmer and engineer.

Algorithms & Data Structures

Former senior engineer at a major French tech company who returned to academia to share what he learned building high-scale systems. His algorithms class is famous for its challenging problem sets that actually prepare you for technical interviews. When not teaching, he's contributing to open-source graph processing libraries.

Active Research Areas

Our faculty publish in top venues, maintain industry collaborations, and bring cutting-edge knowledge into the classroom.

▶

Artificial Intelligence

From fundamental machine learning research to applications in natural language processing, computer vision, and reinforcement learning. Faculty collaborate with industry partners on real-world AI problems, and students actively contribute to research projects as research assistants.

▶

Cybersecurity

Network defense, cryptography, and vulnerability research. The department maintains a dedicated cybersecurity lab where students practice attack and defense scenarios in a controlled environment. Industry partners sponsor research projects and recruit directly from our security track graduates.

▶

Software Engineering & Distributed Systems

Research into software quality, distributed architectures, and developer productivity. With connections to major French tech companies, our faculty work on problems like how to test complex distributed systems, how to improve code review effectiveness, and how to build systems that scale gracefully.
